Ready-to-Use iOS & iPadOS Full-Screen App with Native Share Sheet & Bambu .3MF Export

🍎 1. Open on Your iPhone or iPad & Add to Home Screen

LIVE iOS / iPadOS APP READY
Live iPhone & iPad App Link (HTTPS):
https://sensorybridge.github.io/lithostudio-3d/

Scan the QR code with your iPhone or iPad Camera to launch directly in Safari, then tap Share (↑) → Add to Home Screen.

SCAN WITH CAMERA
  1. On iPhone or iPad: Open https://sensorybridge.github.io/lithostudio-3d/ in Safari (or scan the QR code above).
  2. Tap Share ↑ → tap "Add to Home Screen" → tap Add.
  3. It installs with the custom Litho Studio 3D medallion icon and launches full-screen on both iPhone (with interactive bottom sheet + bottom dock) and iPad (Split-View CAD workspace + Apple Pencil support).
  4. Tapping Share .3MF or Download STL opens the native iOS / iPadOS Share Sheet so you can send directly to Bambu Handy, Files, or AirDrop!
