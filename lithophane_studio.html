🍎 1. Open on Your iPhone Right Now & Add to Home Screen

LIVE HTTPS URL READY
Live iPhone Web App URL (HTTPS):
https://sensorybridge.github.io/lithostudio-3d/
  1. Open https://sensorybridge.github.io/lithostudio-3d/ (or your Google Cloud Run URL) in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button ↑ at the bottom of Safari.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" → tap Add.
  4. Launch LithoStudio 3D from your iPhone Home Screen — exporting `.3MF` or `.STL` automatically opens the iPhone Share Sheet so you can save to Files or open in Bambu Handy!
