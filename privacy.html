Privacy Policy — LithoStudio 3D

Effective Date: November 8, 2026

1. Zero Photo or Personal Data Collection

LithoStudio 3D processes all photos, 2D greyscale depth maps, and 3D meshes (.STL, .OBJ, .3MF, and .JSON slicing profiles) 100% locally on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac using on-device WebGL and HTML5 Canvas.

  • Your photos are never uploaded to any external server or cloud database.
  • We do not collect, store, share, or sell any personal data, photos, usage analytics, or device identifiers.
  • No account, sign-in, or subscription is required.

2. Photo Library & File Access Permissions

When you select a photo in LithoStudio 3D, the image is read directly into local device memory solely to generate your 3D lithophane or keepsake relief model. When you close or refresh the app, temporary image data in memory is cleared immediately.

3. App Store Privacy Nutrition Label

For Apple App Store Connect declarations: "Data Not Collected" — the developer does not collect any data from this app.
